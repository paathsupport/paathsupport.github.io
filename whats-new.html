RELEASE NOTES

What's new

What each version of PAATH brought, newest first.

1.0.0

October 27, 2026
  • Android Auto runs at 1080p, and the car's picture changes size during the drive (1080p, 720p or 480p) as the link allows, without restarting Android Auto or losing your navigation.
  • A picture change, UNDO and the connection test take effect at once, with no restart.
  • During a call the picture goes to 720p and stays smoother (15 fps).
  • The picture-change line on the car's screen fills and goes by itself.
  • The car page recovers by itself if it gets stuck while loading.

0.4.182

October 24, 2026
  • Setup walks you through the hotspot and Android Auto, and on Samsung guides you to a routine that turns the hotspot on with the car.
  • A connection test on the car's screen picks your picture; steady drives move it up.
  • No location permission: auto-stop uses the car's Bluetooth.
  • Navigation voice during calls (Settings, Car).
  • Without signal, the car still finds PAATH for up to a minute.

0.4.179

October 17, 2026
  • Easier to read: lighter gray and larger text across the app.
  • At 1080p on a weak connection, the picture steps lower before it breaks up. If even that is too much, PAATH switches to 720p for the rest of the drive (never right after a call) and says so on the car's screen. You can turn this off in Video & Sound.
  • Samsung hotspot guide: on phones where PAATH can't read the band, tap DONE once you have set it.

0.4.177

October 16, 2026
  • Samsung phones: a step by step guide to the best hotspot setup for your car, in HELP. The steps sit in your notifications and tick as you set them.
  • If your car can't find the phone's 5 GHz hotspot, PAATH offers the guide.
  • On a weak connection the car's picture now holds still for a moment instead of breaking up into green blocks.

0.4.176

October 16, 2026
  • PAATH now speaks Hebrew. It follows your phone's language, or pick one in Settings, Language.
  • A guide for turning on Android Auto's head unit server, with a picture and the steps in a notification.
  • The setup gets your certificate for you.
  • Hold the car address on Home to copy it.
  • Updates come from Google Play, like your other apps; PAATH no longer asks by itself.

0.4.170

October 13, 2026
  • Fixes PAATH saying "Android Auto is on another screen" after STOP and START when no other screen was connected.
  • After STOP and START, the car screen catches up within seconds. It could keep an old message, like ANDROID AUTO IS NOT READY, over a working picture for up to a minute.

0.4.166

October 13, 2026
  • If Android Auto is already on another screen in the car, PAATH now waits and says so, then connects by itself once that screen lets go.
  • Opening PAATH's car page in a second browser moves the picture there. The first one offers TAKE BACK.
  • PAATH now tells you when a new version is ready on Google Play.
  • About lists the open-source licenses PAATH is built on.

0.4.160

October 11, 2026
  • PAATH's screens no longer show mirrored on phones set to a right-to-left language.
  • A new Support PAATH page in Settings: donate, share PAATH, send your log, or leave feedback on Google Play.
  • About shows the support email. Tap it to write.
  • Help now tells Google Maps' "Phone needs to cool down" apart from PAATH's "Phone is hot" banner.

Something not right? In the app, open Settings, Support PAATH and tap Share Log. More fixes are on the Help page.
