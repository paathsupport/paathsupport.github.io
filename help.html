BASICS

How PAATH works

Your phone runs Android Auto as if it were plugged into a car. PAATH takes the picture and sound and sends them to your Tesla's browser over your phone's hotspot. You use Android Auto on the car's screen while the phone stays in your pocket.

One time setup
  1. Open Android Auto settings, scroll down and tap Version ten times to turn on developer settings.
  2. Scroll back up, tap the three dots at the top right and choose Start head unit server.
  3. In PAATH, tap START. If Android asks to allow a VPN, allow it. PAATH uses it only to give the car a fixed address to reach; your internet traffic does not go through it.
  4. Turn on your hotspot and connect the car to it.
  5. In the car's browser, open the address PAATH shows on its home screen.
  6. Pairing your car in PAATH is optional. It lets PAATH start on its own when you get in. To have it stop when you get out too, turn on Stop when I leave the car in Settings, Car.

In the app: the ANDROID AUTO SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

Best hotspot setup
  • Use 5 GHz if your phone offers it and your car connects to it.
  • Otherwise use 2.4 GHz and set the broadcast channel to 11.
  • SAMSUNG The broadcast channel setting only appears when Wi-Fi sharing is off.
  • OTHER PHONES Look for a channel or broadcast channel option in your hotspot settings. Not every phone has one.
  • Keep mobile data on. The car leaves a hotspot that has no internet within a second.

In the app: the HOTSPOT SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

What is normal
  • The picture stops while you reverse. The car gives the screen to the cameras, and the picture comes back after.
  • Music plays about one second behind. That cushion keeps it smooth through short hiccups.
  • The navigation voice starts about half a second late, for the same reason.
  • During a phone call the picture gets softer on purpose, so it keeps moving.
  • The phone gets warm. Most of the heat comes from Android Auto, the hotspot and Bluetooth, not from the picture quality.
  • If Stop when I leave the car is on, PAATH stops about 25 seconds after you leave.
  • For a sharper picture, raise the bitrate in Settings, Video & Sound. The frame rate does not change how much the connection carries.

TROUBLESHOOTING

No picture, or Android Auto does not open
  1. Unlock your phone. Android Auto may be waiting for you. Tap PAATH's notification if you see one. Do Not Disturb can hide it.
  2. If PAATH says Android Auto is not responding, clear all your recent apps, then open Android Auto settings and turn on Start head unit server again. Stopping and starting PAATH does not fix this.
  3. If your phone restarted or Android Auto was closed, check that Start head unit server is on. It does not turn itself back on.
  4. Make sure Android Auto is up to date. Very old versions ignore PAATH.

In the app: the ANDROID AUTO SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

The page does not load in the car
  1. Check that the car is connected to your phone's hotspot.
  2. Check that mobile data is on. The car leaves a hotspot without internet within a second.
  3. Check that PAATH is started.
  4. Use the address shown on PAATH's home screen.
  5. After updating PAATH, reload the page in the car once.

In the app: the HOTSPOT SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

The picture stutters or freezes
  1. Turn off the car's Bluetooth media audio. It made the biggest difference in our tests.
  2. Use 5 GHz if your phone and car allow it. Otherwise set the broadcast channel to 11.
  3. SAMSUNG Turn off Wi-Fi sharing first, or the broadcast channel setting stays hidden.
  4. Do not reload the page during a freeze. It recovers on its own.
  5. On 2.4 GHz, short freezes can happen during phone calls. The picture gets softer on purpose to ride through them.

In the app: the BLUETOOTH SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

In the app: the HOTSPOT SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

The sound is choppy or stops
  1. Turn on Opus audio in Settings, Video & Sound.
  2. The steps for a stuttering picture help the sound too.

In the app: the VIDEO & SOUND SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

The car drops off the hotspot during a call

This can happen on 2.4 GHz. The car reconnects on its own, usually within a few minutes.

The picture is cut off or has black bars
  1. Fill screen in Settings, Video & Sound shapes Android Auto to your screen. It is on by default.
  2. On a phone or tablet, after you rotate the screen, wait about two seconds for Android Auto to adjust.

In the app: the VIDEO & SOUND SETTINGS button on the HELP page.

No sound when this phone is the screen

PAATH plays the sound through this phone's speaker by itself. While Android Auto runs, it takes other apps' sound, so the browser stays quiet on purpose.

"Phone needs to cool down" message

This message comes from Google Maps, not from PAATH. Waze does not have this limit. PAATH lowers the picture quality on its own when the phone gets truly hot.
